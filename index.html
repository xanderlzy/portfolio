About

Hi, I’m Xander. I study Film, Sound & Video at Ngee Ann Poly’s School of Film & Media Studies, and I’m happiest with a camera in my hands.

I’ve also held the boom, dressed the set, sat in the edit and graded until the timeline stopped looking like a timeline. Knowing what every department goes through makes me better in mine, and easier to work with on set.

Outside school I shoot events for RS Photography on my own Sony FX30. I’ve also been a Venture Scout since 2021, so yes, I turn up prepared.

Right now I’m looking for an internship with a production house, ideally in the camera department.
